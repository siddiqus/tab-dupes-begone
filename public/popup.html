Tab Dupes Begone

Already open? Switch to it instead of opening a duplicate.

Switch to the open tab for…

What never switches
  • Right-click a tab → Duplicate.
  • Reopening a closed tab (Cmd/Ctrl-Shift-T).
  • A page opening a tab by itself, without your click (window.open).
  • Typing a URL into a tab already showing a page — Chrome’s own “Switch to tab” suggestion handles that.

Matching is by exact URL: …/repo and …/repo#readme are different pages.
